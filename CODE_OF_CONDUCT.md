# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We commit to
acting in ways that keep the community open and welcoming.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the community:

- being considerate and respectful of differing viewpoints,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments,
- personal or political attacks,
- publishing others' private information without permission,
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that do not align with them.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **https://codeberg.org/OpenAPIHQ/OpenAPILoggingMiddleware/issues**. All reports
are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers will respect the privacy
and safety of anyone who reports an incident.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1; see the link above
for the complete terms and enforcement guidelines.
